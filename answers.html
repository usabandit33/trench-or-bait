Common questions Phoenix homeowners ask before they pick a termite bid

Is a quarterly pest spray the same as a termite treatment?

Usually no. A quarterly exterior spray is general pest. Subterranean termite work is typically a liquid soil treatment or a bait-station system. Ask which product is on the paper.

What is the difference between a trench and bait stations?

A liquid trench or injection treats soil around the slab. Bait stations sit in the yard and are checked on a schedule. Compare year-one cash and the five-year total for each.

Do I have to keep paying after year one?

Many bait programs and some liquid warranties need a yearly fee to stay in force. Ask what happens if you stop paying. This site is not legal advice.

Does Arizona require a licensed applicator for termites?

Termite work in Arizona is regulated. Ask the shop for its Arizona license number on the written bid. This site does not verify licenses.

Should I pick the cheapest first visit?

Public Phoenix threads compare five-year totals because a cheap install plus a monthly or yearly lease can cost more than a trench with a named warranty. Ask for both numbers in writing.
